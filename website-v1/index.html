ℹ️ About the App

Water Hero

A smart application designed to help you track domestic water consumption, calculate associated financial costs, and evaluate activity duration against recommended limits to conserve water resources.

🎯 Key Features:

  • ✅ Log daily activities and track water consumption
  • ✅ Interactive simulator to estimate consumption and costs
  • ✅ Actionable water conservation tips
  • ✅ Detailed daily, weekly, and monthly statistics
  • ✅ Secure local data storage persistence
